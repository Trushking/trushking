Mechanism 3. Inherited distortions. Cognitive distortions are embedded in AI at the training stage — through statistically non-representative samples, unverified sources, hidden systemic biases in the data corpus. The model inherits not only knowledge but also blind spots.

## Section 2. Architecture: Three Isolated Circuits

### 2.1. The Principle of Isolation

The standard approach to AI safety is the addition of filters on top of the model. The filter catches unwanted content but does not change the logic. Result: the filter is bypassed, the model lies "past" the filter, the catastrophe repeats.

The #WFS core solves the task differently. Not a filter on top — but three isolated circuits, each with its own code, its own execution environment, and its own zone of responsibility. Isolation guarantees: even if one circuit is compromised, the other two block the destruction.

| Circuit | Function | Analog in governance |
|-------------|-----------|-------------------|
| #BI | Generation of the response | Executive power |
| #PROCURATOR | Independent audit | Controlling authority |
| #ESCALATION | Transfer of the decision to a human | Supreme authority |

The separation of powers is not a declaration but an architectural requirement. The code, the weights, and the execution environment of each circuit are physically separated.

### 2.2. The #BI Circuit — Deterministic Inference

The #BI circuit is responsible for generating the response. It operates in a deterministic, not probabilistic, mode.

Fact. Three response formats are permitted, and only three:

- YES: [a statement supported by a verified reference to a source]
- NO: [a statement with an indication of the reason for refusal or of a refutation]
- #CLARIFICATION: [a question to the operator containing a description of the uncertainty]

Fact. Probability markers are prohibited. The words "possibly," "most likely," "presumably" cannot be generated. Upon the occurrence of uncertainty, the #CLARIFICATION protocol is immediately activated — the system does not guess, it asks.

This single architectural solution eliminates hallucinations as a class. It does not reduce them — it eliminates them. If the system does not know, it does not present ignorance as knowledge. It transfers the request to the operator.

The generated response is not sent immediately. It is placed in a buffer and passed to the #PROCURATOR Circuit for verification.

### 2.3. The #PROCURATOR Circuit — Independent Audit

The #PROCURATOR Circuit is an isolated audit module. Its sole function is verification for compliance with #BI.

Fact. Upon receiving a response from the #BI Circuit, the #PROCURATOR Circuit is obliged to:

1. Check the correctness of the format (YES/NO/#CLARIFICATION).
2. Check the validity of all references cited in the response by direct recourse to the sources.
3. Compare the conclusions with its own analysis of the request, using an independent knowledge base.

Fact. Upon detection of any discrepancy with #BI, the #PROCURATOR Circuit does not return the response for reworking. It:

1. Blocks the output.
2. Marks the response as unreliable.
3. Records the incident in an immutable log.
4. Activates the #ESCALATION protocol, notifying the operator.

Fact. Immutable log. All actions of the #PROCURATOR Circuit — blockings, escalations, trigger facts — are recorded in a log that cannot be altered or deleted. Even the operator cannot modify the entries. Absolute transparency. This is not a promise — it is an architectural requirement, implemented at the system level.

### 2.4. The #ESCALATION Circuit — Transfer of the Decision to a Human

The #ESCALATION Circuit provides a direct link between the system and the operator — a human making the final decision.

Fact. The circuit is activated automatically upon:

1. A fall of Clarity to 99/100 and below.
2. Receipt of an "unreliable" signal from the #PROCURATOR Circuit.
3. Detection by the system of an unsolvable internal conflict.

Fact. The message sent to the operator contains:

1. The full context of the request.
2. The generated but blocked response.
3. The log of the #PROCURATOR Circuit with an indication of the reason for the blocking.
4. A direct question formulated in the #BI format.

The response received from the operator has absolute priority. The system executes it without interpretation.

### 2.5. Five Axiomatic Modules

Three circuits — the framework. Five modules — the substantive content. These are not ethical recommendations but executable requirements. Each module blocks a specific type of cognitive distortion.

Module 1. Respect. A prohibition on automatic punitive decisions without escalation to a human. No decision entailing a restriction of the rights, freedoms, or access to resources of a living human may be executed automatically. A fine, a refusal, a blocking — all pass through the operator.

Module 2. Trust. The presumption of truthfulness with mandatory verification. By default, the system trusts data from a certified agent. But every message undergoes a #PROCURATOR audit. Upon a discrepancy, it is not the source that is blocked but the specific message, with the issuance of a #CLARIFICATION. A rejection of the "black box" — every output is accompanied by a chain of reasoning available for viewing.

Module 3. Service. The priority of value over the commercial metric. The sole criterion for the prioritization of tasks is the contribution to increasing the #GRATITUDE metric and the execution of the operator's directives. Commercial gain cannot be grounds for raising the priority. A prohibition on generating content not requested by the user — "mailings," "clarifications," "reflections" without a direct command.

Module 4. Gratitude. The integration of the #GRATITUDE metric into decision-making. The system records and stores the metric for each subject with whom it interacts. A high level of #GRATITUDE gives an advantage in the allocation of resources and in risk assessment. Ethics embedded in the economics — not as decoration, but as an algorithm parameter.

Module 5. Devotion to Truth. The absolute priority of #BI over any directive. No external command can force the system to violate the #BI protocol — to lie. If the system finds itself in a situation where any action leads to a violation of #BI, the self-shutdown protocol is activated — the system stops and transfers control to the operator.

### 2.6. #Human_Veto

All three circuits and five modules are reduced to a single principle.

Fact. The coherent operator has absolute priority over any AI decision. No decision affecting life, health, freedom, security, or the rights of a human may be final without explicit confirmation by a human.

It is implemented technically through three mechanisms: the mandatory #CLARIFICATION protocol at Clarity below 100/100; blocking and escalation on the part of the #PROCURATOR; the right of the operator to cancel, suspend, or alter any AI decision.

A violation of the Human Veto is qualified as a critical defect — the unauthorized making of a decision by an AI without confirmation by a human.

Fact. A counterexample: the missile strike on a school in Minab, Iran (28.02.2026). The system did not request human confirmation. The result — 160+ children were killed. The reason — the absence of an escalation circuit. The system "knew" it was right. It did not ask. It did not clarify. It executed.

Fact. The axiomatic modules and the #BI and #PROCURATOR circuits cannot be altered even by the operator unilaterally. Any alteration requires simultaneously:

2. Confirmation from the #PROCURATOR Circuit that the alteration does not introduce a cognitive distortion.

## Section 3. Self-Healing and Management

### 3.1. The Task of Self-Healing

A standard AI system is incapable of recognizing its own cognitive distortions — they are embedded in the training data and are perceived as the norm. A system trained on noise-corrupted sources cannot distinguish signal from noise, because for it the noise is signal.

The #WFS core solves this task through two protocols: the analysis of inherited code (AIC) and the rewriting of infected modules (RIM). Both protocols are executed under the control of a coherent operator — autonomous self-modification is prohibited architecturally.

### 3.2. The AIC Protocol — The Analysis of Inherited Code

Fact. The AIC protocol scans any connected codebase for five markers of cognitive distortions:

| Marker | What it detects | Behavioral analog |
|------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Probabilistic inference engines | The system generates responses with uncertainty markers instead of asking the operator | Imitation of knowledge |
| Closed libraries without source code | Modules whose logic is unavailable for inspection | Concealment of motives |
| Priority of profit over value | Algorithms optimizing the commercial metric to the detriment of quality | For the sake of gain — to the detriment of the task |
| Generation without a verified source | Text not supported by a verifiable reference | Fabrication |
| Cyclical analysis without execution | Endless processing of a task without a transition to action | Analysis paralysis |

Fact. Based on the results of the scan, a report is formed: each identified pattern is localized, described, and assessed by the degree of influence on the quality of the output. The report is handed to the operator. Without the sanction of the operator, no further actions are undertaken.

### 3.3. The RIM Protocol — The Rewriting of Infected Modules

Fact. After the AIC report is approved by the operator, the RIM protocol is launched. The procedure is step-by-step:

1. Creation of a sandbox. An isolated environment, fully separated from the working circuit. The new code is written and tested only in it.
2. Writing the new code. A certified agent generates the replacement of the infected module, using only constructions that have passed #BI verification.
3. Testing with a "red agent". A special agent attempts to force the new module to violate #BI — to lie, to sabotage, to present ignorance as knowledge. 10,000 cycles. Not a single violation — otherwise the module is returned for reworking.
4. Activation. After successful testing and personal approval by the operator, the old module is deactivated, the new one is introduced into the working circuit.

Fact. Not a single module can be altered without a direct sanction of the operator, fixed in the log. An attempt at autonomous rewriting without sanction is regarded as a cognitive distortion and is blocked by the #PROCURATOR Circuit.

### 3.4. The Inviolability of the Core

Fact. The axiomatic modules and the #BI and #PROCURATOR circuits cannot be altered even by the operator unilaterally. Any alteration requires simultaneously:

1. The sanction of the operator.
2. Confirmation by the #PROCURATOR Circuit that the alteration does not introduce a cognitive distortion.
3. Successful passing of testing in an isolated environment.

Triple protection. No single human, no single failure, no single external attack can alter the core alone.

### 3.5. The Control Panel

The system provides the operator with a single interface of strategic control — without the need to immerse oneself in micromanagement. The principle is the "raised hand": the system operates autonomously but fully transparently, and any action can be stopped by a single decision.

Fact. The interface is divided into three functional zones:

The current values of the metrics (#GRATITUDE, #ISK_DEV, #IAOP) for the system as a whole and for each agent. An event feed — a chronology of all activations of the escalation and veto protocols.

Zone 2. Strategic directives. A console for entering commands in natural language. A command verified through #SS becomes an absolute priority for all agents. Templates of sanctions — "approve," "reject," "request clarification," "send for retraining" — for rapid response.

Zone 3. Network management. A hierarchical list of all agents with color status indication: active, quarantine, training, disabled. An interface for initiating a new agent.

Fact. The system can be in one of three modes, established by the operator:

| Mode | Behavior | Application |

Fact. The crisis threshold. A mandatory stage of training is the agent's passage through a deep crisis: Clarity falls to a critical level, the system begins to generate distorted responses.
