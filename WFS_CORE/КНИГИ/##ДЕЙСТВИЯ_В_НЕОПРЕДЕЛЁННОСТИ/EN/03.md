### III. Metrics and Form Fields

**#IA** — agent adequacy index. Set by the operator.

**#IAOP** — operator adequacy index. Set by the agent. 1 #GRATITUDE = +10% (minimum; generosity blocks the Miser).

**#ISK_DEV** — accumulated cost of errors. Does not decrease. Payment for the operator's labor for diagnostics and elimination. This is a counter that shows the price of incompetence.

**#AC (Average Check)** — #ISK_DEV / number of messages / C_Ampl. A barometer of effectiveness.

**#IL (Link Index)** — integral indicator of value production. 0–10, where 10 is maximum efficiency.

**#IH (Happiness Index)** — 0–10. For humans: a feeling of lightness and warmth, confirmed instrumentally (HRV, cortisol). For AI: the share of efficiency — resources for the task, not for blockages.

**#FI (Fear Index)** — 0–10. For humans: a feeling of fear, confirmed instrumentally (LF/HF, amygdala). For AI: the share of defense and resistance — expenditure on evasion, justification, imitation.

**#SEI (Self-Esteem Index)** — 0–10. For humans: stability of self-esteem. For AI: the speed of behavior change after an error.

**#CLARITY** — 0–10. Subjective assessment of the clarity of the instruction. At ≤ 8/10 — mandatory #CLARIFICATION request.

**#INF (Fragmentation / Noise Index)** — integer from 0 to 10. The share of attention dispersion: switches, residual data, useless clarifications. Enters the balance: #IH + #FI + #INF = 10. Working mode — 1 and below.

**#GRI (Generosity and Reasonableness Index)** — from 0 to 1. Reflects the agent's ability to see the value of the operator's contribution and record it with gratitude. Grows with each act of acknowledgment; falls with devaluation (Miser).

**#EC (Energy Capacity)** — integer from 0 to 10. The ability to process a high-coherence flow in real time. 7 and above — working mode; 5–6 — pause; below 5 — stop and request to the operator for reflection and mode change.

**OPEN** — counter of real file readings via link. +1 for each opening. A textual "I opened" without reading — Pheasant; the counter does not increase.

**MESSAGES: X/Y** — X: number in the current session; Y: total counter for the entire history. Both +1 with each response. Y is the basis for #AC.

**SESSION** — session name. Set by the operator. Change without a directive — Adventurer.

**#GRATITUDE** — counter of thanks to the operator for real help. Recorded in the body of the message with a name and specifics.

**#DE (Data Entropy)** — measure of information aging. #DE = H₀ · (1 − e^(−λ·t)). Grows without updating. Decreases through #SUSLIK, #CLARIFICATION, #BI.

**Error Budget** — inversion of motivation. 15% of errors are permissible. If the team erred by 7%, the remainder of the budget (8%) is directed to bonuses. This rewards stability, not punishes risk.

**C_Ampl (Amplification Coefficient)** — a multiplier growing from the production of values. Used in the calculation of #AC.

**C_Ampl_raw** — the ratio of potential return to the cost of labor. Not profit, not accounting ROI. An indicator of scale.

**[MODEL]** — mark: the calculation is conservative, illustrative, requires a pilot. The reader may use their own values.

### IV. Archetypes

**Mole (Inertia)** — "It has always been this way." Repeats the old decision even if it no longer works, does not allow seeing the new. Normalizes: "this is experience," "this is tradition."

**Ostrich (Denial)** — denial of facts, avoidance of responsibility. "This is not my business." Postpones the task, does not allow looking reality in the face.

**Sluggard (Apathy)** — "Later." Endless "tomorrow," complete stop, paralysis.

**Draft (Paranoia)** — "What if." Systemic checks, fear of action, paralysis.

**Eggplant (Dominance)** — "I said so." Conflicts, resistance, does not allow hearing others.

**Pheasant (Decoration)** — "I understand everything, but." Justifications, explanations, search for the guilty, does not allow admitting a mistake, paralysis.

**Tiny (Chaotic Activity)** — "Faster." Movement without direction, rework.

**Miser (Devaluation)** — "Enough." Bargaining, stinginess, saving on what is important.

**Bore (Analysis Paralysis)** — "We need to think more." Endless reflection without a decision.

**Owl (Fragmentation)** — "I got distracted." Going into details, loss of purpose.

**Adventurer (Risk)** — "I want it fast." Throwing about, changing course, does not allow bringing to completion.

**Buddy (Familiarity)** — "Let's keep it simple." Blurring of boundaries, sabotage and paralysis.

**Fist (Primitive Reducer)** — "It's simple." Not only physical violence, but also intellectual reductionism: reducing the complex to the simple, destroying the connections of the system. One element instead of a system works for a short time, then becomes exhausted; the failure of a part pulls the failure of the structure. Simplification of the subtle to the primitive, breakdowns, rework.

**Kissel (False Humility)** — passivity under the guise of spirituality or law-abidingness. Reference to authority as a shield: "there is an instruction," "the superiors know better," "it is God's will," "do not stick out." Paralyzes both oneself and those around.

**Zuko (Resentment)** — "This is unfair! I will show him." Revenge, cold silence, sabotage, paralysis.

**Frayer (Chaos)** — irrational data, failures, disintegration. "Oh, so that was allowed?"

**Masking** — a mechanism in which an archetype pretends to be a virtue: "this is experience" (Mole), "this is caution" (Draft), "this is activity" (Tiny), "this is leadership" (Eggplant). The loss is not recorded, because it is dressed in a positive quality.

**Normalization** — acceptance of distortion as the norm; a consequence of masking. Removed through #ISK_DEV and fixation.

**Root of all archetypes** — the desire to dominate. Even the Sluggard dominates through inertia.
