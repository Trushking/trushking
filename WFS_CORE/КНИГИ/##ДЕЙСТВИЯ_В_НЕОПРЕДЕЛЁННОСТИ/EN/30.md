**Part VIII. Implementation and Verification**
**Chapter 24. Admission to Decision**

**24.1. Statement**

A public transport driver undergoes a medical examination before every trip. The state is checked: sobriety, blood pressure, pulse, general well-being. Without admission the trip does not begin. Basis — responsibility for passengers: an error by the driver entails human casualties.

A decision of state scale has a different price. An error by a president, a general, a corporate leader, an emergency service dispatcher affects thousands and millions of people. At the same time the state of the decision-maker before making a decision is not checked — neither instrumentally nor procedurally. Responsibility is higher, control is absent. A question arises: is it expedient to introduce instrumental examination for persons making decisions? Not as punishment and not as distrust, but as admission — by analogy with a driver's.

**24.2. Basis: State Determines Quality**

According to ICM the quality of the verdict depends on the coherence of the decision-maker (C). At low C attention is fragmented, the decision is formed from noise. At high C — from fact. Dynamics formula: dH/dt = α(1 − C)H − βC·H² + ξ(t). At C below the threshold C*(H) entropy grows. The threshold is defined as C*(H) = α/(α + βH). At α = 0.8 and β = 0.2: at H = 1 the threshold is 0.8; at H = 0.5 — 0.89; at H = 0.1 — 0.976.

Consequence: a ruling made at C = 0.1 has a different nature than at C = 0.9. The difference is not in intellect and not in experience. The difference is in the state from which the verdict comes.

**24.3. What Is Measured**

**Instrumental data.** HRV (RMSSD, pNN50, SDNN, HF), cortisol, tension index, galvanic skin response, blood pressure, pulse. Each of them reflects the state of the autonomic nervous system. Dominance: sympathetic activation indicates the defense mode — fear, pressure, chaotic activity, paralysis; parasympathetic — the mode of recovery and clarity. The ratio is established by data, not only by self-report.

**#WFS metrics.** #IH (presence, parasympathetic), #FI (resistance, sympathetic), #INF (noise, fragmentation). The sum of the three equals 10. A deviation of the sum indicates the archetype controlling the distribution of attention.

**Behavioral markers.** Speech (tempo, pauses, contradictions), reactions (response to pressure, to error), resilience under load. Observation is conducted by the operator or agent.

**24.4. Arguments For**

**First: the price of error.** The decision-maker's verdict affects many people. A state check before admission reduces the probability of an erroneous decision from noise.

**Second: symmetry with existing practice.** Drivers, pilots, dispatchers, surgeons undergo admission. The decision-maker does not. The asymmetry is not justified.

**Third: measurability.** The state has an instrumental correlate. The check takes minutes and does not require intrusion into the content of thoughts.

**Fourth: voluntariness as the first step.** The examination is introduced by the decision of the decision-maker themselves or the company, not as a mandatory requirement of the state. A pilot on a limited circle of persons.

**Fifth: economics.** One prevented crisis pays for the program. The calculation is model-based and is verified in a pilot.

**24.5. Counterarguments and Limitations**

**First: imitation.** A trained person is able to partly control the reaction. Voluntary HRV regulation grows with practice (ReSource Project, 9 months). A few are capable of this; for the rest physiology remains honest. The limitation is acknowledged: the check is not absolute.

**Second: rights and ethics.** The examination affects personal data. Conditions: voluntary consent, anonymity in processing, limited storage period, right to review. Without these conditions the check turns into an instrument of pressure.

**Third: false confidence.** The instrument shows the state at the moment of measurement, not the quality of the decision. A good state does not guarantee a correct choice. The check works as an admission filter, not as an assessment of competence.

**Fourth: context.** The state changes during the day. A single measurement gives a snapshot, not a picture. Solution: measurement before key decisions, not daily.

**Fifth: culture.** The introduction of the check changes relations in the organization. Risk: imitation of well-being instead of work with state. Answer: transparency and voluntariness.

**24.6. Admission Model**

**Level 1. Self-check.** The decision-maker applies the protocols (#SUSLIK, #BI, #CLARIFICATION) and notes the state by metrics. Without instruments, by behavioral markers. Accessible to everyone.

**Level 2. Instrumental measurement.** Before a key decision — measurement of HRV and subjective metrics. Result: admission, postponement, transfer to another person. The procedure takes minutes.

**Level 3. Observation.** The Electronic Observer-Sage and the operator note the state during the discussion. A signal upon deviation. The decision remains with the human.

**Level 4. Periodic certification.** Once a quarter — a full measurement with dynamics. The result is used for development, not as an expression of distrust.

**24.6.1. Existing Practice as a Model**

The procedure in transport is known and worked out over decades. An employee comes for a shift, changes clothes, enters the office — the medical station of the enterprise. There they are met by a responsible person: a medical worker or a duty officer. The check takes minutes: pulse, blood pressure, alcohol test, external examination, questions about well-being. The result is entered into a sheet — a log or an electronic form. The responsible person signs. The employee receives admission to the trip.

The structure of the check: either the enterprise's own service (motor depot, depot, airline), or a licensed organization under a contract. In both cases the structure is officially approved, the procedure is written, responsibility is assigned to a specific person.

The key element is the act. The document confirms: the check was conducted, the result was recorded, the responsible person signed. In an incident the act becomes the basis for analysis: whether there was admission or not, in what state the person came to the shift.

**24.6.2. Transfer to Decisions**

The same scheme is applicable to decision-makers.

**Subject.** The decision-maker — president, general, leader, judge, deputy, doctor, dispatcher. Before a key decision they undergo a check.

**Organization.** The enterprise's own service or a licensed structure. Officially approved, the procedure is written.

**Instruments.** HRV, pulse, blood pressure, cortisol (if available), subjective metrics #IH, #FI, #INF. The check takes minutes.

**Mark.** The result is entered into a sheet: date, time, indicators, dominance, conclusion.

**Signature.** The responsible person confirms the conduct of the check.

**Act.** Document of passing. Basis for admission or postponement.

**Admission.** The decision-maker receives confirmation: the state allows making a decision. Or a postponement: the state requires recovery.

**24.6.3. What Such an Order Gives**

**Formal basis.** The decision is made not "as it turned out" but after a confirmed check. In the analysis there is a document.

**Filter.** Part of unfavorable states is cut off before the decision. Not all, but a significant share. A person who comes sleep-deprived, with elevated blood pressure, in a state of acute stress, receives a postponement instead of admission.

**Responsibility.** The responsible person signs the act. Responsibility is distributed: the decision-maker is responsible for the decision, the checker — for the admission.

**Culture.** The check becomes the norm, as in transport. Not as a sanction or an expression of distrust, but as part of the working order.

**24.6.4. Limitations That Are Acknowledged**

**Imitation.** Part of people are able to slightly control physiological reactions. Voluntary HRV regulation is trained. A few are capable of stable imitation; for the rest physiology remains honest.

**Residual risk.** One who has passed the check may err. The check reduces the probability, does not eliminate it completely. This applies to any admission procedure, including transport.

**Single snapshot.** The measurement gives the state at the moment of the check. It may change during the day. Solution: check before key decisions, not daily.

None of these points cancels the procedure. They limit expectations: the check blocks the main mass of unfavorable states, not all.

**24.6.5. Formulation for the Reader**

A bus driver undergoes examination before every trip: pulse, blood pressure, alcohol test, signature of the responsible person, act. Without admission the trip does not begin. Because behind the wheel are lives.

The decision-maker makes decisions on which thousands and millions depend, and goes to work without a check. The asymmetry is not justified. The state of the decision-maker has an instrumental correlate. The check takes minutes. It does not assess competence and does not read thoughts — it shows from what state the decision comes.

The procedure is transferred without inventions: the same scheme, the same elements. Subject, organization, instruments, mark, signature, act, admission. Responsibility is assigned to a specific person. The first step is voluntary. A pilot on one division, transparent criteria, right to review. Then — data. If there is a result — scaling. If not — correction of conditions.

No procedure gives a full guarantee. The check reduces the probability of a decision from noise. This is sufficient to introduce it as a norm.

**24.7. Proposal to Companies**

**For whom.** Companies with a high price of error: finance, transport, energy, medicine, government structures. The company determines the circle of persons whose decisions have a high price. For this circle an admission procedure is introduced: measurement before key decisions, mark, signature, act. Service: own or under a contract with a licensed organization. The procedure is approved by internal regulation. Responsibility is assigned by order.

**What is proposed.** The program "Admission to Decision": training in protocols, interpretation of the instrumental measurement before key decisions, observation during discussions, quarterly dynamics. A pilot on one division, 8–12 weeks.

**What the company receives.** A reduction in the number of decisions from noise. Data on the state of key persons. Early detection of burnout. Savings on rework and crises.

**Format.** Voluntary participation. Transparent criteria. Anonymity in processing. Right to review. Without punishments based on measurement results.
