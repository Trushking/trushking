**Chapter 5. Coherence and Balance of Attention: From Question to Measurement**

**5.1. The Question That Has No Answer**

Key decisions in a company are made by specific people: the board of directors, top management, heads of divisions. Their choice determines billions, the fates of employees, the stability of the business. At the same time, the state from which these people make decisions is not checked in reporting, in regulations, or in control systems. Everything is checked: financial indicators, deadlines, product quality. The state of the one who forms these indicators remains outside the field of view.

A bus driver undergoes examination before every trip: pulse, blood pressure, signature of the responsible person, admission act. Behind the wheel are the lives of passengers. The decision-maker has higher responsibility, and no admission procedure exists. This asymmetry became the starting point of the #WFS laboratory's work.

**5.2. What Is Measured: Coherence**

The International Laboratory for Consciousness Studies and Attention Defragmentation investigates how the state of attention affects the quality of decisions and develops tools for managing this state. The subject of study is coherence: the degree of integrity of attention. The value is measured in the range from 0 to 1. In the average human the value stays within 0.05–0.15. At such a level a small share of attention resource is directed to the task; the rest is spent on internal noise: anxiety, defensive reactions, thinking about the past, waiting for the future.

The result is known to every leader: rework, drawn-out approvals, decisions made in haste and revised a week later, the departure of key employees. All of this is attributed to the human factor. Behind it stands a measurable parameter.

The laboratory works in three directions. Research: the connection of coherence with the entropy of the environment is described mathematically; the dynamics equation shows at which value of the parameter losses grow and at which they decrease. The model is verified on data from more than twenty AI agents and on two biological subjects with instrumental confirmation. Diagnostics: scanning of work communications — video, audio, text — to identify zones where decisions come out of noise. Result: a map of model losses with a price in money and a forecast. Training: protocols that increase coherence. Not motivational programs and not relaxation trainings, but a sequence of actions with a verifiable result.

**5.3. Difference from Existing Offers**

The market contains many solutions, and each works with its own layer. Time-management trainings teach how to distribute time. Coaching helps to set goals. Well-being programs reduce stress. Consulting optimizes processes. Automation removes manual operations. All the listed approaches work with the process or with the symptom: the schedule is optimized, the interface is designed, the regulation is introduced. A person enters an improved process in the same state in which they were before.

#WFS works with the root — with the state from which the decision comes. The process is important, the interface is important, the regulation is important, and all of this is preserved. A layer is added that until now has not been measured. Three differences determine the position.

**First — instrumentality.** The state has a physiological correlate: heart rate variability, cortisol, galvanic skin response, behavioral markers. The measurement takes minutes and does not depend on self-report. Suggestion does not change physiology; training does — this is confirmed by randomized studies.

**Second — verifiability.** The offer does not require faith. A pilot: one group, three months, measurements before and after, a control group. The result is compared by numbers. If there is an effect — scaling; if not — data.

**Third — targeting.** Diagnostics precede training. The program is built for the specific loss zones of a specific company, not offered to everyone in the same form.

**5.3.1. What Already Works in the World**

**HeartMath.** Method: HRV biofeedback, the Quick Coherence technique — transferring attention to the heart area, slow breathing, evoking a positive feeling. The measurement takes one minute. Applied in production: high-tech line (22% fewer defects), customer service (50% less stress, savings of £423,000), pharmacy chain (40–71% fewer errors), medical nurses (42% improvement on the burnout scale), police (reduction of stress symptoms, increased performance).

Cases: medical workers (stress reduction, increased resilience), engineering firm (improvement of well-being and quality of life). There are no direct production indicators (defects, errors, turnover) in the cases.

**Conclusion.** Both instruments prove that work with state in production gives a measurable result. #WFS differs in its object: not training of physiology, but elimination of the archetypal cause of losses in communication and decisions.

**5.4. Why Coherence Is the Prospect**

There are three grounds. First: the source of losses has been found and measured. Companies invest for years in processes, equipment, automation, and hit a ceiling. The ceiling is set by the state of the decision-makers. While the parameter was not measured, losses were attributed to circumstances. Now there is an opportunity to work with the cause, not with the effect.

Second: the market exists but does not cover the task. Companies working with coherence in production are known. HeartMath applies HRV biofeedback in stressful environments and has measurable results: 22% reduction of defects on a production line, 40–71% reduction of errors in a pharmacy chain, 50% reduction of stress and reduced turnover in customer service. Muse uses EEG neurofeedback to train attention and sleep; it has no direct production cases and is applied in the office. Both instruments work with training of state. There is no diagnostics on the market tied to the economics of losses and correction protocols. The laboratory occupies precisely this niche: not training of state, but identification of the source that destroys it.

Third: the effect is multiple with small investments. This is not about capital construction or equipment purchase. Investments are concentrated in training and diagnostics. The return is measured in multiples: with an increase in coherence from 0.07 to 0.25–0.30, the throughput of attention increases several times with the same staff, the same processes, the same conditions. Companies that introduce work with the state of decision-makers first gain an advantage that is difficult to copy: another structure can purchase the same equipment but cannot purchase someone else's coherence.

**5.5. Three Channels of Attention**

The full state of consciousness at each moment is ten units, or one hundred percent. Attention is indivisible: it is either directed into one of three channels or distributed among them. The three channels are described by three metrics.

**#IH** — presence in the present. Parasympathetic, coherence, contact with fact.

**#FI** — resistance to reality. Sympathetic, fear, threat.

**#INF** — noise, fragmentation. Wandering of the mind, probabilistic markers, absence of contact.

All three are measured on one scale — in whole units, where one unit equals ten percent. Fractional values create an illusion of precision and hide the gap. A whole number is more honest: it immediately shows how many tenths of the resource went into each channel.

The three states are mutually exclusive by nature. One cannot simultaneously be in contact with fact and in fear of it. One cannot be in fear and in noise without fear. Therefore the sum is not arbitrary arithmetic but a balance: how much of the attention resource went into presence, how much into resistance, how much into noise.

**5.6. Diagnostics of the Gap**

Normally the sum equals ten. This means that the entire resource is described, no part has leaked into a hidden channel. A deviation of the sum from ten is a gap, and it is diagnostic.

If the sum is less than ten, part of the resource is not accounted for: hidden fragmentation, devaluation (the Miser understates #IH and #IAOP), displaced fear (the Ostrich), or imitation of calm (the Kissel). If the sum is more than ten, the resource is overstated: duplication, imitation of presence (the Pheasant), attribution to oneself of a state that does not exist (the Eggplant).

The gap is not a measurement error. It is a marker of the archetype that at the given moment controls the distribution of attention. When the gap is visible, #SUSLIK-#SAGE records it without involvement: "the sum is nine — one unit is missing, where did it leak?" The #AXE cuts off: either find the hidden channel or acknowledge that it does not exist. Generosity does not allow the Miser to understate #IH. Persistence consolidates the habit of counting honestly.

**5.7. Criterion**

At the output this gives not an ideal product immediately, but a measurable purity of perception. When the sum is stably equal to ten, the decision is made by reality, not by the noise around it. #INF falls, #IH grows, #FI falls, and the product becomes better not because there is more data, but because there are fewer obstacles between reality and the decision.

The criterion is not completeness of information but balance of attention. The sum is not a report. The sum is a diagnosis. Example of balance: nine units of presence, zero resistance, one unit of noise. Total — ten. The gap is closed honestly, not by adjustment.

**5.8. Where Work Begins**

With diagnostics. Before changing anything, it is necessary to see the actual position: where exactly, in which zones, in what volume losses arise. Diagnostics gives a map. With the map, a basis appears for the next step — targeted training where it is necessary and in the volume determined by fact.
