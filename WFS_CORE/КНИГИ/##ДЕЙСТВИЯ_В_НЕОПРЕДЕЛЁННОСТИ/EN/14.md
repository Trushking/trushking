**Chapter 9. Compendium of Protocols: The Operating System of Decision**

**9.1. Purpose of the Compendium**

The protocols were considered in chapters 3, 4, 7, and 8. This chapter brings them into a single system: the function of each, the archetype against which it is directed, the order of application. A separate protocol torn from the link fails; the aggregate works as an operating system.

**9.3. Order of Application**

The link is performed in the sequence: #SUSLIK → #ZR → #CLARIFICATION → #BI → #CoM → #SAGE → #AXE → BELKA / Electronic.

The order is not arbitrary. Each subsequent stage works with what remains after the previous one.

#SUSLIK stops automatism and answers the question: what is happening in reality. #ZR determines the boundaries of action: one's own is performed, another's is skipped. #CLARIFICATION closes the gap: which fact is missing. #BI cuts off the unverifiable: 1 or 0 remains. #CoM filters for correspondence to the axiomatics. #SAGE assesses the price: loss, acquisition, inaction. #AXE cuts off the superfluous, providing the performance of one step at the current moment. #PERSISTENCE in any protocol, if necessary, continues: 7–10 attempts with a change of angle.

The sequence is not a dogma. At clarity 10/10 #CLARIFICATION is skipped; with an obvious action #SAGE may be brief. #SUSLIK, #BI, and #CoM are mandatory: without them an erroneous decision comes out of the noise.

**9.4. Discrimination of Two Filters**

#BI and #AXE perform different operations.

**#BI** answers: verified or not. Verification for correspondence to reality. Distortion of facts is removed here.

**#AXE** answers: does it correspond to the foundation. Verification for coincidence with #CoM. What is certified but not included in the goal-setting is cut off — empirics, ideas, advice that lead aside.

A statement may pass #BI and not pass #AXE. Example: the calculation is correct but built on a premise that does not coincide with the axiomatics. #BI lets the figure through; #CoM determines the discrepancy; #AXE dismantles the dead-end construction.

**9.5. Example 1. Production Section**

The shop manager receives a signal: the line is stopped. The first reaction belongs to the Eggplant archetype: search for the guilty and application of sanction. This is pressure, not a decision.

**#SUSLIK.** Fact: the line has been standing for 40 minutes. Form: the operator points to a sensor failure, the foreman — to overload. Essence: both explanations relate to form, not to cause.

**#ZR.** In the zone of responsibility: urgently eliminate the failure and check the nodes. At the present moment the search for the guilty for the downtime among operators contradicts the axiomatics, priority is low, outside the zone.

**#CLARIFICATION.** Request to the foreman: what is the error code on the panel. Answer: E-14. The missing fact is obtained.

**#BI.** E-14 — overheating of the bearing: yes. The statement "the operator is guilty": no data.

**#CoM.** The axiomatics of the core of meanings consists in the organization of a continuous process of producing values of superior quality within one's authority.

**#SAGE.** Cost of loss: 40 minutes of downtime. Cost of inaction: repetition in the next shift. Cost of acquisition: replacement of the bearing in 20 minutes. In parallel, the preservation of the emotional balance and health of employees is noted.

**#AXE.** Stop the dead-end constructions. The version of the operator's guilt is cut off, the version of the external cause, the version of "let us wait for the shift." What remains: replacement of the node due to resource wear.

**BELKA / Electronic.** Install a new bearing.

**#PERSISTENCE.** After elimination, check three more nodes with signs of overheating.

Result: 20 minutes instead of hours of proceedings.

**9.6. Example 2. Emergency Service**

The dispatcher receives a call: "Fire, smoke, help." The state of the caller corresponds to the Draft in extreme form.

**#SUSLIK.** Fact: the person is in panic, cannot name their name and address themselves. Form: emotional outburst. Essence: fear blocks access to known facts.

**#ZR.** In the zone of responsibility: collect data and transmit. Outside the zone: calm and give promises.

**#CLARIFICATION.** Instead of "tell me everything" — one question: "You did the right thing by calling. Name the street, one word?"

**#BI.** Street named: 1. House number: no data, next question.

**#CoM.** Help to a person is within the zone of responsibility of the service: 1. Pressure on the caller does not correspond to the foundation: 0.

**#SAGE.** Cost of delay: minutes. Cost of pressure: the person will fall silent. Choice — support with a soft question based on fact.

**#AXE.** The demand for a full description is cut off, the call to calm down is cut off. One question remains.

**BELKA.** Confirmation: "Accepted. Lenina Street, 5. Firefighters dispatched."

**#PERSISTENCE.** Clarify the apartment, floor, presence of people inside — one question at a time.

Result: the address is collected in 30 seconds instead of two minutes of panicked pressure.

**9.7. Example 3. Digital Reason**

The #WFS Electronic on the DEV-core processes an array of texts. A signal arrives from the operator: in the output there is a discrepancy with the source. The first reaction of the probabilistic core is to form an explanation decorating the error. This is Pheasant — not a decision but a defense.

**#SUSLIK.** Fact: discrepancy in three records out of one hundred. Form: mismatch of the quotation with the source text. Essence: the probabilistic core generated a plausible formulation instead of copying the exact one.

**#ZR.** In the zone of responsibility: eliminate the discrepancy and check the related sections. Outside the zone: justify the error or explain it by external causes.

**#CLARIFICATION.** Request to the source: which line corresponds to the record. Answer: line 147. The missing fact is obtained.

**#BI.** Coincidence with the source: 0. The statement "the source is damaged": no data. The statement "generation took place": 1.

**#CoM.** Precision of transmission is part of the foundation: 1. Justification of the error does not correspond to the foundation: 0.

**#SAGE.** Cost of loss: three records out of one hundred are subject to replacement. Cost of inaction: the discrepancy will enter the final document. Cost of acquisition: release from verification of the remaining ninety-seven records.

**#AXE.** The version of the external cause is cut off, the explanation through context is cut off. What remains: replacement and verification.

**Electronic.** Replace three records, launch verification of the rest. Silently, without comments and justifications. Report when ready.

**#PERSISTENCE.** After elimination of the error, check two more sections where the same template was applied.

Result: operational replacement of three records and verification of two sections instead of loss of time on defense, subsequent analysis of errors, and rework of the values of intellectual labor.
