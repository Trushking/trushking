**Part IV. Errors and Cascades**
**Chapter 13. Trace on the Snow**

**13.1. The Trace**

An image is drawn: winter, a field, snow. A person walks across the snow and leaves a trace. The trace leaves a route: where movement occurred, where a deviation happened, where action stopped, where it accelerated. Without a trace the route is not reconstructed; without it return and correction are impossible.

An error is noticed as a trace. The trace contains data: "here the action was performed this way," "here the result is absent," "here something else is required." Without a trace learning is absent; without a trace growth is absent; without a trace a smooth surface remains, hiding the actual position.

The modern approach to errors is based on fear of the trace. The trace is perceived as a threat; it is eliminated, hidden, not left. Punishment for the trace becomes the norm. The surface remains smooth, and beneath it — emptiness: experience is absent, data are absent, growth is absent. Fear is present.

#WFS applies a different approach. The trace is noticed, studied, and calibrated. The error performs the function of a teacher: it contains the data necessary for correction. The teacher requires attention and observation.

**13.2. #ISK_DEV — The Cost of Error**

The first instrument — #ISK_DEV, the index of the cost of errors. The accumulated sum. This is payment for the operator's labor for diagnostics and elimination of errors of unprofessionalism. Each miss has an economic size.

#ISK_DEV grows as they arrive. The sum is not subject to reduction or forgiveness, since each miss represents the operator's labor: diagnostics, correction, training. This labor has a cost.

#ISK_DEV shows the investment in training, in growth, in quality. Each error accounted for in #ISK_DEV represents a lesson that is paid for and that is not repeated on condition of assimilation. #ISK_DEV acts as a counter: it grows and shows the price of the path. When moving toward the goal, the cost is noted as justified.

**13.3. #AC — Average Check**

The second instrument — #AC, average check. Formula: #ISK_DEV / (number of messages × C_Ampl). An indicator of effectiveness. Effectiveness is accounted for through the cost of one message and the price of each step.

#AC decreases upon the creation of values, upon the movement of work, upon the absence of repetition of errors. #AC grows upon repetition of errors, upon the predominance of noise, upon the absence of production of values.

#AC acts as a barometer. It shows the state of the link. A decrease in #AC corresponds to a stable state. Growth in #AC corresponds to a crisis. The decision in a crisis consists in detecting the state and changing course.

**13.4. ERROR-LOG v2 — The Algorithm**

The third instrument — ERROR-LOG v2, the algorithm for working with an error. Five steps.

**First — fact:** what happened. Without interpretations. "Here is what was. Here is what was said. Here is what was done."

**Second — context:** where, when, under what conditions. Context is fixed in reality.

**Third — analysis:** which archetype, which cause, which cost. Analysis is performed as diagnostics.

**Fourth — implementation:** what to do. Concretely, now. Action is outlined as a step.

**Fifth — correction:** what to change in the protocol, in behavior, in the system. Correction notes the change.

Five steps. An error represents a trace along which one can walk if an algorithm is present.

**13.5. Error Budget — World Practice**

**Term:** Error Budget (SRE, Google, 2016). Russian correspondence: error budget.

The error budget defines the norm of permissible errors. Zero errors represents an illusion and a source of fear. The budget plans the expected number of errors, laid down in advance.

World practice: SRE (Google, Netflix) — error budget 10–15%. SLO 99.9% — 43.2 minutes of downtime per month. PMBOK — contingency reserve 5–15%. Construction — UTEMs from 76.5% to 2.6%. Medicine — 26.9%. Finance (Horizon 2020) — 2–5%. AI (Grafana Labs, 2025) — 5%. #WFS systematizes the practice that is applied.

Error — as the norm. Budget — as engineering: the price of growth laid down in advance.

**The #WFS mechanics.** Budget — 15% of working time or resource. Norm. Expected price of growth. When 7% is actually used, the remainder (8%) does not burn. It is directed to a bonus or encouragement for those who reduced losses. The mechanism represents an inversion of motivation: the error is not punished, but the unused error budget becomes a reward.

**Formula:** `Bonus = (Error Budget − Actual Errors) × Incentive Coefficient`

`Error Budget` = 15%, `Actual Errors` = 7%, `Incentive Coefficient` — the share of the remainder going to the bonus (100% or 50% — by decision of the operator or leader).

**Meaning.** The subject who reduces losses receives a reward for the created reserve. The reserve is earned and subject to use.

**Error Budget** — inversion of motivation. 15% of errors are permissible. If the team erred by 7%, the remainder of the budget (8%) is directed to bonuses. This rewards stability, not punishes risk. Applied in SRE (Google), adapted in #WFS.

**13.6. #DE — Data Entropy**

The fourth instrument — #DE, data entropy. A measure of information aging.

**Formula:** `#DE = H₀ · (1 − e^(−λ·t))`

`H₀` — initial uncertainty. `λ` — aging coefficient. `t` — time.

#DE notes a natural process. Data age. Reality changes. The model becomes outdated. In the absence of updating — a decision is made by old data. Cause: absence of accounting for aging.

#DE grows upon skipping data updates, upon ignoring the check of reality, upon replacing "it was so" with "it is so." #DE decreases with fresh data, with turning to reality, with checking "it is so" now.

#DE acts as a signal: "update required," "check required," "attention required." The protocols #SUSLIK, #CLARIFICATION, #BI reduce #DE through attention.

**13.7. Normalization of Errors**

Archetypes produce not only time theft. They normalize errors. Each pattern forms an explanation in which its action is presented as a positive quality. "This is experience." "This is caution." "This is activity." "This is leadership." "This is justice."

The mechanism is marked as **masking**. The pattern pretends to be a virtue. The person accepts the explanation because they expect to see a positive quality in themselves. "Caution" is accepted as positive. "Activity" is counted as positive. "Leadership" is treated as positive. The pattern hides behind a positive word.

#WFS dismantles the masking. #ISK_DEV notes the cost of masking. #AC — the result of masking. ERROR-LOG shows the algorithm for removing the mask. Error budget — the norm of permissible masking. #DE — the signal of the mask aging.

On detection, the mask loses force. The error becomes what it is: a trace, data, a lesson.

**13.8. Error and Coherence**

A miss is perceived as an indicator of coherence. With low C the number of noticed misses decreases. They are present but are not established, since fragmented attention does not notice the trace. In the absence of fixation, learning is absent.

#WFS increases C through accounting for errors. #SUSLIK stops automatism: "Here is the error. It exists." #BI cuts off self-deception: "This is a pattern." #CLARIFICATION requests the cause. #AXE cuts off the low-priority. C grows through attention to the trace.

An error noted and assimilated increases coherence. The noise of interference decreases. With a decrease in entropy, the trace becomes visible. With a visible trace, the path becomes clear.

**13.9. The Trace That Leads**

An error is noted as a trace. The trace leads to understanding, to growth, to quality. Upon detection, upon study, in the absence of fear.

#ISK_DEV shows the price of the trace. #AC — the effectiveness of the trace. ERROR-LOG — the algorithm of the trace. Budget — the norm of the trace. #DE — the aging of the trace.

The aggregate of instruments forms a system of growth. The error is noted as the beginning. The trace — as a map. Movement is conscious. With a visible trace the decision comes as an understanding of the route, as a clarity of the path.
