**Chapter 14. Cascade**

**14.1. Domino Tiles**

A familiar image: a table, on the table — domino tiles lined up in a row. The first is small, behind it the second, the third, the twentieth. From the side — a toy, nothing serious. One tile falls and touches the next, that one — the next, and in a second the whole row is down.

The first tile was small. A cascade is determined not by weight but by connection. One error launches the next, that one — the next. At the moment the row falls, the first tile is no longer visible; the last is noticeable. But the root is at the beginning.

Titanic, Minab, and the DEV-tank are described as cascades. They represent a chain in which each link was a point available for a break. The break was not performed because the chain was not visible: attention held the link, and the chain slipped away.

**14.2. Titanic: Eleven Links**

Night, Atlantic, ice, ship, "unsinkable." Eleven links of the cascade are known.

**First.** Rivets of low-quality steel, brittle at low temperatures. Supply error. Root.

**Second.** Watertight bulkheads of insufficient height. Water overflowed the top. Design defect.

**Third.** Lifeboats for 1,178 people with 2,224 on board. Outdated regulation.

**Fourth.** Evacuation drills were not conducted. Basis — confidence that they would not be needed.

**Fifth.** Seven iceberg warnings ignored. Mole: "it has always been so, we made it."

**Sixth.** The captain drove the ship at dangerous speed for a record. Adventurer: risk for glory.

**Seventh.** The lookouts had no binoculars. Sluggard: "we will manage."

**Eighth.** The bridge was isolated from the lookouts. Eggplant: "the captain knows."

**Ninth.** The radio operator replied on air: "Shut up, I am busy." Fist and Buddy, aggression and familiarity at a critical moment.

**Tenth.** The radio operator of the neighboring ship was asleep, did not receive the SOS. Ostrich: "not my business."

**Eleventh.** Catastrophe. 1,500 dead.

Each of the listed links is noted as a point at which the chain could have been stopped by one action. The stop was not performed. Basis: attention held a separate link, and the sequence of links remained outside the field of view. The chain became visible after closure, when the first link had already left perception.

**#SUSLIK:** stop, see. **#BI:** "Is there an iceberg? Yes. Do we reduce speed? No. Why?" **#AXE:** "Stop. Reduce. Strengthen observation."

The system, struck by archetypes, did not do this. Basis — not absence of knowledge. The cascade was already running, and each link normalized itself: "it is accepted," "nothing terrible," "we made it before."

**Discrimination of Three Modes**

The use of force is described by three modes, and each has its own order of actions.

**First mode — established enemy activity.** Equipment, manpower, movement, fire are detected. Action: escalation to a human. The machine establishes the fact and transmits the decision; the human confirms the use of force. #Human_Veto acts in full.

**Second mode — an attack that has occurred.** The strike is already being delivered. Action: repulsion. Delay means loss. Here the order is different: the means of destruction are applied to an established target, the decision is recorded and transmitted to the human simultaneously with the action. #BI establishes the fact of the attack; #SS shows the decision and time.

**Third mode — no activity, data incomplete.** Neither equipment, nor manpower, nor movement is detected. Action: the use of force is prohibited until human confirmation. #CLARIFICATION is performed mandatorily.

**14.6. General Structure**

All cascades have one structure.

**First link** — small, unnoticeable: "it is accepted," "nothing terrible," "we made it before," "looks like a threat," "help." A drop.

**Second** — normalization of losses. Each link explains itself: "this is experience," "this is caution," "this is activity," "this is decisiveness." The mine pretends to be a virtue, and the person believes because they want to believe.

**Third** — absence of a break. No one stopped, asked, checked, or said: "Stop. This is not it." The chain was not visible; the link was visible.

**Fourth** — catastrophe. Lawful, since the cascade reached the end. The break was not performed.
