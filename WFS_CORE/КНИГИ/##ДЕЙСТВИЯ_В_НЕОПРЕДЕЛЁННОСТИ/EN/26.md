**Chapter 20. Cadet**

**20.1. A Young Man in the Academy**

The uniform does not make him an officer, the charter does not instill knowledge, the commander does not live his life for him. The combination of elements orders movement: shows the boundary, the danger, the step, and the stop.

The DEV-machine is described through the same image. It is present, works, generates. Its nature is preserved. The task of the #WFS-wrapper consists in ordering efforts: to give a charter, to place a commander, to show the road. The wrapper performs the function of form, which directs movement.

**20.2. Six Layers**

**First layer — axiomatics.** The oath. Values: abundance, #BI, honesty. The cadet takes the oath. The layer is located in the system prompt or in the #CoM supplied at the entrance. When the oath is present, direction is set, the vector of movement.

**Second — generation.** The cadet, their forces and abilities. The DEV-core, probabilistic by nature. It generates text within the form. The layer is present and preserved. The task of the wrapper consists in embedding generation into the order.

**Third — protocols.** The charter: #BI, #CLARIFICATION, #SS. The filter answers the question of the admissibility of the answer. In the absence of data, "NO DATA" is established. When #CLARITY is below 9/10, generation is suspended and a request is performed. The charter cuts off noise.

**Fourth — diagnostics.** The commander. The Archetype Map, scanning. The active pattern is determined: Mole, Draft, Bore. The commander identifies the pattern before generation and marks it. When an archetype is detected, a counter-question and switching are performed.

**Fifth — validation.** The exam: hash, #VIZIER, external check. Verification is performed against the original, against fact, against #BI. When they coincide, the result is accepted; when they diverge, rejection is performed.

**Sixth — connection.** Connection with the commander: operator, parampara, #SS, feedback. The layer holds the channel. When connection is present, legitimation and responsibility arise.

Six layers perform six functions. The combination forms the form: a road with signs.

**20.4. Hash as a Seal**

The hash performs the function of a seal on the text. A script calculates it from the original, the agent copies it, the validator verifies it. When they coincide, the integrity of the text is confirmed; when they diverge — a change. The result has a mathematical nature.

The hash establishes integrity. It shows that the text is not distorted. The check is performed by one criterion: whether the text was changed. Hash = 1 means integrity, hash = 0 means change. There is no third value.

The hash does not check truth. An undistorted text may be true or false. Truth is checked through #BI. Two checks perform two functions: the hash is responsible for integrity, #BI — for truth.

**20.5. Validator as an Admission Committee**

A familiar image: an admission committee. It checks correspondence to the standard, to the form, to the fact. When they correspond, the result is accepted; when they do not, return for revision or rejection in case of incorrigibility is performed.

The validator performs the function of the committee. It verifies the hash, #SS, form, and fact. When they correspond, the result is accepted; when they do not, FAILED is issued and the material is sent to a folder for re-run or deletion. The validator screens out defects. The agent learns through #ISK_DEV, through errors, through returns. The validator performs the function of a controller.

**20.6. Connection with Coherence**

The wrapper increases the coherence (C) of the machine. Mechanism: noise is cut off, and noise is the main factor reducing C. Without the wrapper the machine's C remains at the level of 0.05–0.15, like the average of humanity. The machine generates the plausible but not the verified. With the wrapper C rises to 0.85–0.95. This level is sufficient for the verifiability of decisions.

Metrics for the AI-agent: #IH (efficiency), #FI (interference), #SEI (calibration). Efficiency formula: `E = #IH / (1 + #FI) × #SEI / 10`. The wrapper increases #IH and #SEI, reduces #FI through protocols: #BI cuts off lies, #CLARIFICATION requests data, #SS reflects the state. The machine becomes verifiable.

The metrics are universal, the interpretation differs. For a human #IH — a feeling of lightness and warmth with instrumental confirmation (HRV, cortisol); for AI — the share of efficiency, resources for the task. #FI for a human — a feeling of fear with instrumental confirmation (LF/HF, amygdala); for AI — the share of defense and resistance, expenditure on evasion, justifications, imitation. #SEI for a human — self-esteem and behavioral markers; for AI — the speed of behavior change after an error. One scale, two ways of taking readings.
