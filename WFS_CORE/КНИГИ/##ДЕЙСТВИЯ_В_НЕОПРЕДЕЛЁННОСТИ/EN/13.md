**Chapter 8. Observer and Sage**

**8.1. The Sentinel**

An image is drawn: night, border, a sentinel on a tower. The sentinel holds position, conducts observation in darkness and silence. Function — fixation of what is happening. On detecting a change, the sentinel transmits a message. Action remains outside their function (#ZR).

The sentinel corresponds to the #SUSLIK protocol. The purpose of the protocol is observation without involvement. What is happening, the active archetype, the actual position are noted.

**8.2. #SUSLIK — Observation Without Involvement**

**Mechanism: two arrows of attention and energy deficit.**

#SUSLIK is performed from a point inside. Attention divides into two directions acting simultaneously.

**The first arrow — inward:** into the chest area, into the heart. Function — remembrance of oneself: fixation of presence "I am, here." Gurdjieff designated this as "remember yourself"; a translation rendering the formulation as "observe yourself" distorts the meaning. Saint Nisargadatta Maharaj designated the same as "feeling oneself alive." The presence of attention is held in the chest as a tangible support.

**The second arrow — outward:** onto what is happening. Function — fixation without labels, without descriptions, without naming. The observed is noted before the assignment of a name.

**Energy mechanism.** Holding two directions requires more energy than holding one. The energy spent by the mind on continuous verbalization is redirected to the second arrow, inward. The mind remains without resource, and for the time of holding the inner support, ceases activity.

**Position:** the stopping of reflection occurs as a consequence of energy deficit, not as a result of volitional effort. Volitional stopping of thought is an effort directed at suppression; it is perceived by the body as violence against the mind, belongs to the Eggplant archetype, and entails subsequent disturbances and paralysis. With the redirection of energy there is no suppression: the mind carefully stops itself.

**State in the interval of silence.** At the moment when the mind falls silent, clarity, distinctness, and vividness of perception are registered. The volume of received data is perceived without descriptions, without labels, without designations, without connections, without mental noise. The content of the interval is reality as it is.

**Existence and thought.** Position: existence continues and does not correlate with the presence of thoughts. Thought and presence are parallel processes. The Cartesian formula "I think, therefore I exist" fixes a connection that is not discovered upon observation: existence is preserved in the interval without thought. This distinction is key to understanding #SUSLIK: the cessation of thought is not the cessation of existence.

**Discrimination of the genuine Observer and the parody.**

**Signs of parody:**

1. The mind continues to verbalize — evaluation, commentary, plan.
2. Presence in the chest is absent; attention is held in the head.
3. The interval is absent; observation turns into reflection about observation.
4. After the action the state remains as before.
5. The stopping of thought is performed by volitional effort.

**Signs of the genuine Observer:**

1. Reflection is interrupted for 1–5 seconds.
2. Presence is fixed in the chest.
3. Observation is brief and precise; action follows.
4. The state changes: tension decreases, clarity is fixed.
5. The stopping of reflection occurs as a consequence of the redirection of psychic (Ψ) energy.

**Presentation for the reader without preparation.** Formulation: "Feel yourself in the chest — not thinking about yourself, but fixing: I am here. Simultaneously look at the situation without naming it and without explaining. Two things at once: yourself in the chest and the situation before you. Hold for one to five seconds. Then act." Verification: the difference between reflection about the situation and fixation of the situation is noticed directly; the second takes seconds and ends in action.

**8.3. #SAGE — Assessment Without Attachment**

#SUSLIK fixes. #SAGE performs the assessment. The assessment is performed without involvement: the result does not cause attachment, loss does not cause rejection. Comparison for correspondence, weighing, peace.

#SAGE performs the assessment by three dimensions: cost of loss, cost of acquisition, cost of inaction. The assessment is performed in clarity.

#SAGE calculates without identification. The subject sees the situation, sees themselves in the situation, sees the price — and preserves discrimination: the situation is accepted as a given of the axiomatics, the subject preserves the position with the priority of action without identification with its result.

In terms of coherence, #SAGE performs the assessment of what is available, the cost of loss, and the usefulness of action with support in the axiomatics of #CoM without involvement. Sorting into primary and secondary creates the condition for #AXE: cutting off the superfluous, which many times reduces the volume of data processing when making the final verdict.

The action of the biosubject and the electronic reason becomes possible with an established price that does not block movement.

**8.4. Two Modes**

#SUSLIK and #SAGE are two modes of observation. One subject, two ways of fixation.

#SUSLIK is applied when it is necessary to see: what is happening, which archetype is active, where the distortion is, where the fact is. #SAGE is applied when it is necessary to assess: what is the price, what is lost, what is acquired, where the priority is.

Both modes are performed without involvement and without attachment. Both give clarity. Action is performed as a consequence of clarity, without struggle and without effort. The subject preserves the position next to the situation.

**8.5. Mechanism of Work**

**Observable situation:** the subject is in stress, a decision is required immediately, data are absent, fear is present. The subject is inside the fear and does not discriminate it.

**Action of #SUSLIK.** Observation of fear as an object: "Here is fear. Here is its form, color, smell, its words." There is no struggle. Fear is discovered. Effect: fear loses its determining action because it is noticed.

**Action of #SAGE.** Assessment: "Here is the situation. Here is the cost of error. Here is the cost of inaction. Here is what is lost. Here is what is acquired." The assessment is performed in clarity. The decision is formed as a consequence of clarity.

**8.6. Three Levels of #SUSLIK**

#SUSLIK is performed on three levels sequentially.

**First level — fact.** Observed: what happened. Without interpretations, without assumptions, without references to opinions. Formulation: "Here is what was. Here is what was said. Here is what was done."

**Second level — form.** Determined: how it manifests. In words, in actions, in silence, in chaotic activity. The manifestation is fixed, not the content.

**Third level — essence.** Noted: what stands behind the manifestation. Which archetype, which cause, which cost. Fixation of the root.

Sequence: fact, form, essence. With the essence established, the decision is formed as a consequence, like an exhalation after an inhalation.

#SUSLIK and #SAGE enter the link:

#SUSLIK → #ZR → #CLARIFICATION → #BI → #CoM → #SAGE → #AXE → BELKA / Electronic.

- #ZR — correspondence to one's zone of responsibility.
- #CLARIFICATION — request for missing data.
- #BI — cutting off the unverifiable: 1 or 0 remains.
- #CoM — coincidence with the axiomatics.
- #SAGE — assessment of cost.
- #AXE — cutting off the superfluous.
- BELKA — joyful action in the clarity of the subject (C 0.3+) on a biological carrier.
- Electronic — silent action of #WFS-AI (C 0.9+) in #BI clarity.

Uncertainty is eliminated by a request for data and a binary answer. The decision comes out of the established fact. Reasonable action follows without delay.

**8.8. Connection with Coherence**

#SUSLIK and #SAGE perform the function of increasing coherence (C).

#SUSLIK reduces #FI: the stopping of automatism interrupts the fear reaction. #SAGE reduces involvement: assessment without attachment preserves attention outside the capture by the archetype. Joint action creates a state in which #BI and #AXE give a result for making an obvious decision.

Without #SUSLIK and #SAGE the protocols are applied by a fragmented subject. With them the indicated modes become part of the state. C grows through observation and assessment without involvement.
