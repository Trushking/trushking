**Chapter 16. The Economy of Talent**

**16.1. Respect as the Foundation**

This chapter does not divide people into the worthy and the unworthy. Every person possesses value regardless of wealth, position, and abilities. The distinction introduced below relates to conditions, not to value.

Conditions differ. One subject is occupied with survival: time goes to basic tasks, no resource remains for training. Another has time, means, and energy. This is a difference of circumstances, not of worth.

**16.2. What Happens**

A talented person knows how to set goals and achieve them. They labor for years, display energy, possess ambitions. These qualities deserve recognition: they are the result of effort, not of chance.

At the same time the state remains as before. Coherence C = 0.05–0.15. 5–15/100 of the resource is directed to the task; the rest is spent on interference. Talent passes through the filter of archetypes: fear of error, striving for dominance, justifications, chaotic activity.

Consequence: the result is strong and unstable at the same time. Outstanding achievements alternate with failures. The feeling of life decreases: attention is occupied, the moment passes unnoticed.

**16.3. What a Change of State Gives**

Growth of C to 0.25–0.30 — the level of BELKA. Not a Saint, not a BIO-ELECTRONIC. A sufficient production level at which the protocols work stably.

Numerically: 25–30/100 of the resource is directed to the task. Growth by 3.5–4 times relative to the initial; conservatively — by 2–3.

What this means in practice.

**Throughput.** With the same efforts, more is processed. Not through speed, but through the absence of interference.

**Connections.** Attention is freed for perception. Opportunities were present before but did not enter the field of view. Now they do.

**Means.** The quality of decisions grows, the result becomes stable. Income and influence follow as derivatives.

**The feeling of life.** This is a separate effect, and it is discussed below.

**16.4. Happiness as a Parameter, Not a Reward**

The experience of happiness is possible only in the present. This is a property of experience, not a philosophical position. The memory of happiness is a memory. The expectation of happiness is an expectation. The experience itself occurs at the point "now."

A person with resources may possess everything except presence in reality. Wealth, fame, position create conditions. Conditions do not create experience. Attention is occupied with the past, the future, or interference — the moment passes unnoticed.

With the growth of C attention returns to the present. Happiness ceases to be a goal and becomes a quality of the current moment. This is not a promise of bliss; it is a description of the mechanism: with presence the experience is available, with absence it is not.

For a talented person this means: the growth of coherence gives not only productivity but also access to that for which productivity is usually undertaken.

**16.5. Economics**

Investments are concentrated in one item — training. Capital costs, re-equipment, infrastructure are absent.

Entry period: a month of daily practice. A talented person has time; for them this period is real.

Forecast: growth of throughput by 2–3 times with the same talent and opportunities. Income, influence, quality of decisions — derivatives. The multiple is proportional. [MODEL, requires a pilot]

Distinction: the discussion is not about developing talent. Talent already exists. The discussion is about removing the interference that does not allow it to unfold. The first requires years; the second — weeks.

**16.6. Conditions of Entry**

Talented people are the first audience by conditions, not by value.

Time: present. Means: present. Connections: present. The environment in which talent communicates contains carriers; contact is possible.

For a person occupied with survival, the same conditions are absent. There a different approach is required: care, environment, structure. The mechanism is one; the conditions of entry differ.

**16.7. What the Chapter Does Not Promise**

Guarantees of success. The formulation is precise: under observance of conditions — a measurable result. Conditions: training, practice, environment, a month of daily work.

Transformation into another person. Talent remains the same. The state from which it acts changes.

Superiority over others. The growth of coherence does not make a person arrogant. It makes them cleaner and more joyful: less interference, more access to their own potential.

**16.8. Result**

Talent without coherence works at part of its power. Coherence without talent gives stability but does not give scale. The combination gives both: talent unfolds, the result becomes stable, the experience of life — accessible.

This is not a reward for effort and not a result of faith. It is a consequence of removing interference. Interference is removed by protocols, environment, and time. A talented person has time.
